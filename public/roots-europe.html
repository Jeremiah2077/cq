  • Morning: Summer Palace (including Kunming Lake boat)
  • Beijing Zoo panda house

Ancient Capital

Learning Focus: History, Art (sculpture), Cultural Studies

Academic Alignment

Learning Focus Areas

Every activity connects to core learning areas. A full Learning Focus document is provided to your school before departure, adaptable to your national curriculum.

Learning Focus Theme How This Programme Connects
History Ancient civilisations & empires Forbidden City, Terracotta Warriors, Great Wall, Silk Road: first-hand encounters with 3,000 years of Chinese history
Art & Design Craft, sculpture & performance Calligraphy, terracotta figurine making, Peking Opera mask-painting: hands-on making and critical analysis
Geography Urbanisation & transport Shanghai skyline, high-speed rail, city wall defence architecture, urban planning: modern and ancient infrastructure
Cultural Studies Identity & intercultural dialogue School exchange, Muslim Quarter, French Concession, hutong home visits: globalisation and cultural diversity
Religious Studies World religions & philosophy Buddhism, Islam in China, Confucianism: Great Mosque, Big Wild Goose Pagoda, Temple of Heaven
Physical Education Active learning & wellbeing Great Wall hike, Tai Chi session, city wall cycling: physical challenge in historic settings

Our Team on the Ground

4-on-1 Education Team

Every China Quest group travels with four dedicated professionals, not one overworked tour guide.

1
Curriculum Designer

Maps every activity to your school's learning objectives. Produces the Learning Focus document and Taskbook debrief guides used before, during and after the trip.

2
Bilingual Education Mentor

Fluent English + Mandarin, trained in youth engagement. Facilitates school exchanges, live translation and cultural interpretation throughout the programme.

3
Safety & Logistics Officer

Manages risk assessments, medical co-ordination and crisis protocols. On-call throughout the trip with escalation procedures aligned to your school's requirements.

4
24/7 School–Family Liaison

Real-time updates to school and parents via a dedicated channel. Primary emergency coordination and pastoral escalation point from departure to arrival.
