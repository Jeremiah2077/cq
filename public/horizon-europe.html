Chengdu
Arrive Chengdu
  • Airport pickup, hotel check-in, rest and adjust
  • Afternoon: Jinli Ancient Street or Kuanzhai Alley free walk
  • Evening: orientation briefing + welcome dinner
2
Chengdu
Panda Conservation Day
  • Morning: Dujiangyan Panda Keeper Programme (clean enclosures, feed pandas, behavioural observation, volunteer certificate)
  • Afternoon: Dujiangyan Irrigation System (UNESCO, 2,200-year-old damless water diversion)

Chengdu → Chongqing
  • Morning: high-speed rail Chengdu to Chongqing
  • Morning: Liziba monorail through building + Cloud-Top observation deck
  • Midday: Ciqikou Ancient Town
  • Afternoon: Shiba Ti traditional neighbourhood
  • Evening: Hongyadong night view + hotpot workshop
4
Chongqing
Smart Manufacturing & Stone Carvings
  • Morning: OPPO Smart Eco Tech Park STEM workshop (robotics exhibition, AI application center, smart home design challenge)
  • Afternoon: Dazu Rock Carvings (UNESCO, 9th–13th century Buddhist/Taoist/Confucian sculptures)
  • Evening: Chongqing drone show (5,000 drones formation performance)
5
Chongqing
Wulong Sinkholes
  • Morning: Wulong Three Natural Bridges (UNESCO, world's largest natural stone arch group) + geology talk
  • Afternoon: Longshuixia Gorge (deep-cut canyon, waterfalls, stalactites)
